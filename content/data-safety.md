This page describes how **blueDog Consulting LLC** ("blueDog," "we," "us") approaches data safety and confidentiality in our consulting work and on this website.

For information about data collected through [bluedog.dev](https://bluedog.dev), see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## Our commitment

We work with organizations on developer relations, community strategy, and developer experience. That work often involves access to internal context, roadmaps, community metrics, and other sensitive business information. We treat that material with care and use it only to deliver the services you engage us for.

## Client and project data

When you engage blueDog for consulting:

- We access only the information needed for the agreed scope of work
- We do not use client materials for unrelated purposes or for marketing without your permission
- We do not sell client or project data
- We limit internal access to people who need the information to perform the work

Specific handling practices (storage location, retention period, subprocessors) can be documented in a statement of work, NDA, or separate agreement when required for your organization.

## Website and contact data

Information submitted through our website contact form is processed by **Netlify** as part of site hosting and form delivery. We use that information solely to respond to inquiries.

We use **Google Analytics** on this site for aggregated traffic insights. Analytics data is separate from client engagement materials and is not used to profile individual visitors for consulting outreach.

## Security practices

We apply reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to a boutique consultancy, including:

- Using reputable cloud providers for website and business tooling
- Limiting access to systems and accounts that hold client or contact information
- Keeping software and dependencies reasonably up to date
- Avoiding unnecessary collection or retention of sensitive data

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to reduce risk and respond promptly if we become aware of a concern affecting your data.

## Third-party tools

Depending on the engagement, we may use common business tools (for example, email, video conferencing, document collaboration, or project tracking). We select tools appropriate to the task and can discuss your requirements before sharing sensitive materials.

## Data retention and deletion

We retain client and project materials for as long as needed to fulfill the engagement and meet reasonable business or legal obligations, unless a contract specifies otherwise. You may request deletion of contact information submitted through this website by contacting us via the [contact form](/contact/).

## Incident response

If we become aware of a security issue that materially affects data you have shared with us, we will notify affected clients as appropriate and work to mitigate the issue.

## Questions

For questions about data safety in a potential or active engagement, or about information submitted through this website, please use our [contact page](/contact/).
