This Privacy Policy describes how **blueDog Consulting LLC** ("blueDog," "we," "us") handles information when you visit [bluedog.dev](https://bluedog.dev) or contact us through this website.

## Information we collect

### Information you provide

When you use our [contact form](/contact/), we may collect:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your company name (optional)
- Whether you would like to schedule a conversation (optional)
- Your agreement to be contacted for informational purposes

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and follow up about our consulting services.

### Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we use **Google Analytics** to collect aggregated usage data such as pages visited, approximate location (country/region), browser type, and device type. This helps us understand how the site is used and improve it.

Our hosting provider, **Netlify**, may also process technical data required to deliver the site (for example, IP address and request logs).

## How we use information

We use the information described above to:

- Respond to contact form submissions
- Communicate with you about our services
- Understand and improve website performance
- Maintain the security and reliability of the site

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us operate this website:

- **Netlify** — hosting, form processing, and related infrastructure
- **Google** — analytics (when enabled)

These providers process data on our behalf to deliver the services above. We do not share contact form information with other third parties for their marketing purposes.

## Data retention

Contact form submissions are retained for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and maintain reasonable business records, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics settings and our internal review practices.

## Cookies and similar technologies

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to collect usage information. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

## Your choices

You may:

- Decline to submit the contact form
- Request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted by contacting us through the [contact form](/contact/)
- Use browser or opt-out tools offered by analytics providers to limit tracking

## Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

## Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our [contact page](/contact/).

For how we protect client and project information during consulting engagements, see our [Data Safety](/data-safety/) page.
